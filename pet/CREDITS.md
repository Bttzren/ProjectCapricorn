# Capri art credits

- `skins/capri/` - her look: standing, blinking, grinning, glitching, sleeping, waking, perched, dangling,
  lying down, talking mouth. Drawn for Capri by **xilia_world00** (Discord).
- `skins/watcher/` - the Watcher, a secret skin. Drawn for Capri by **xilia_world00** (Discord).
- `mini/` - her visor face (chat avatar and tiny mode). Mini art by **xilia_world00** (Discord).
- `faces/`, `cutins/`, `intro/`, `icons/` - Capri expression faces, cut-in panels, intro frames and icons,
  provided by the project owner.
- `spritesheet.webp` - the original Petdex/Codex Capri sheet (the "classic" skin).
- `fonts/ShareTechMono-Regular.ttf` - Share Tech Mono by Carrois Type Design, SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (`fonts/OFL-ShareTechMono.txt`).

A skin is a folder in `skins/` with the same strip names and layout as `skins/capri/`:
240x384 frames for standing strips (idle, blink, blink_glitch, glitch, grin, sleep, wake, dangle, mouth),
384x384 frames for perch strips, and single 3072x1707 lay_* poses.
